## Registration

Please fill out the Google Form, with your time availability preference. We understand you might have multiple commitments for the day. Teams will be allocated based on grade and time match. We'll try our best to get students from the same school work together, but there is no guarantee for it.

## BIOGLOW™

The theme for the year is [CANOPY](https://www.firstinspires.org/first-canopy), and for FLL it's called BIOGLOW™. 

<img src="/assets/img/2026-27/first_bioglow_logo_fll.webp" alt="drawing" width="500" style="display:block;margin:0 auto;" />

[Watch](/news/2026/05/03/first-lego-league-canopy/) the season reveal trailer, also we are going to run as [Founders Edition](/news/2026/06/05/founders-vs-future-edition/) for this season.

### Sessions
**Before the season starts:**

| **August 8** | Innovation Project Research Toolkit for the season mailed to registered students | 
| **Aug - Sep** | Students independently investigate the Innovation Project topic | 
| **September&nbsp;12th** | Students present their topic and investigation for Innovation Project to whole group | 

All in-person sessions are on **Saturdays, (9 AM – 4 PM) &amp; Thursdays, (6 – 7:30 PM)** at Glen Rock High School.
