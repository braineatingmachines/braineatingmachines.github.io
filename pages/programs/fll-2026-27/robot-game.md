Robot Game is one of the three judged components of every FLL season, alongside the Innovation Project and Core Values. Teams build and program a robot to autonomously complete missions on a themed table within a two-and-a-half-minute match.

Watch the current problem video and prepare a short explanation of your robot design ideas, program approach, and strategy.
